# Brand guidelines

## Brand idea

A compass offers direction without dictating one fixed route. AI Engineering Compass brings together timeless software craft and modern AI tools so engineers can make informed decisions.

## Voice

- Practical, clear, and welcoming.
- Curious rather than prescriptive.
- Explain the reason behind recommendations.
- State assumptions, trade-offs, and limits.
- Prefer specific examples over slogans.

## Colour palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm ivory | `#F3EEE5` | Main light background |
| Soft white | `#FFFDF8` | Cards and clear space |
| Sand beige | `#E7DCCB` | Secondary surfaces |
| Charcoal | `#272522` | Primary text and mark |
| Burnt amber | `#C56A32` | Directional accent and emphasis |
| Muted taupe | `#CBB99E` | Subtle dividers and details |

Use charcoal for readable text on light surfaces. Do not use amber for long body copy or small text on beige. For dark surfaces, use soft white text and the amber accent sparingly.

## Logo

- Primary horizontal lockup: `assets/brand/ai-engineering-compass.svg`.
- Standalone mark: `assets/brand/compass-mark.svg`.
- Keep clear space around the symbol. Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, or change the palette arbitrarily.
- Use the standalone mark for avatars and small contexts.
- On dark backgrounds, place the mark on a warm ivory or soft-white panel rather than relying on low-contrast charcoal details.

## Typography

Use a clean sans-serif for headings and body copy. Prefer a readable system font stack in web contexts. Avoid overly condensed or decorative faces.

## Design principles

Warm, calm, editorial, and direct. Use whitespace generously. Prefer simple geometry and clear information hierarchy over decorative technology motifs.
