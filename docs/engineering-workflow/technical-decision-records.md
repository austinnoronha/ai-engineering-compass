# Technical decision records

**Status:** Recommended  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-26

A technical decision record captures a decision that is important enough to explain to future maintainers. It helps teams understand not only what was chosen, but why.

## Suggested format

- **Title:** short decision statement.
- **Status:** proposed, accepted, superseded, or deprecated.
- **Context:** problem, constraints, and relevant forces.
- **Options:** realistic alternatives considered.
- **Decision:** what was selected.
- **Consequences:** benefits, costs, risks, and follow-up actions.
- **References:** supporting sources and related records.

## Keep records focused

Record decisions, not every implementation detail. Update status when a decision is superseded; retain the old record so the history remains understandable.
